Key ideas

You cannot make it happen

Effort is the one thing that reliably blocks it. Hold the phrase lightly, notice whatever is actually there, and let it be uninteresting. It is like falling asleep: you can arrange the conditions, but you can't force the moment.

Say it in your own voice

The phrase is yours, thought silently, not listened to. The recordings only show you the shape — the practice is you thinking the formula, in your head, at your own pace.

Short and often, not long and rare

Ninety seconds, three times a day. Frequency builds the response; duration does not. One long session a day is not the same thing and will not do the same thing.

Nothing happening is normal

Expect nothing for the first few days. Heaviness usually appears somewhere between a few days and two weeks. It may arrive as warmth, tingling, sinking, or the arm feeling larger or further away — those all count.

If restlessness or emotion comes up

That is an autogenic discharge, a recognised part of the process rather than a sign something is wrong. If it is uncomfortable: open your eyes, move your arms and legs, and stop there for that session.

Why bother

Ninety seconds, three times a day, and in about a fortnight you can produce the state without any audio at all — at a desk, on a train, before sleep. It is a skill you keep rather than an app you keep opening.

What the evidence says

A 2002 meta-analysis pooled around sixty controlled trials, with the clearest results for tension headache and migraine, mild high blood pressure, anxiety, and difficulty getting to sleep — broadly comparable to other established relaxation methods. It is part of routine care in Germany. It is not a treatment for a medical condition and not a substitute for one.

Before you practise

Never while driving, cycling, or doing anything that needs your attention. This lowers heart rate and blood pressure — if you have low blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, or epilepsy, speak to your doctor before making it a daily habit.

Always finish with the take-back: bend and stretch your arms firmly, fists clenched; two deep breaths; open your eyes. The one exception is practising in bed to fall asleep — then stay lying down.

What it's been used for

Studied since the 1930s.

A 2002 meta-analysis pooled around sixty controlled trials. The clearest results were for tension headache and migraine, mild high blood pressure, anxiety, and trouble getting to sleep — broadly on par with other established relaxation methods.

People most often come to it for the everyday version of that: coming down after a bad day, getting to sleep, steadying nerves before something that matters.

It is not a treatment for a medical condition and not a substitute for one. If you are being treated for something, this sits alongside that, not instead of it.

The honest deal

Most people feel nothing for the first few days.

That is normal and it is not failure. Heaviness usually turns up somewhere between a few days and two weeks. If you want something that works tonight, this isn't it.

The one counterintuitive part: you can't make it happen by trying. Effort is the thing that blocks it. Your whole job is to say a short phrase to yourself and let it be uninteresting.
