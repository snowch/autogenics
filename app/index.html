    Key ideas

    You cannot make it happen

    Effort is the one thing that reliably blocks it. Hold the phrase lightly, notice whatever is actually there, and let it be uninteresting. It is like falling asleep: you can arrange the conditions, but you can't force the moment.

    Say it in your own voice

    The phrase is yours, thought silently, not listened to. The recordings only show you the shape — the practice is you thinking the words, in your head, at your own pace. In the older books these phrases are called formulae.

    Short and often, not long and rare

    Ninety seconds, three times a day. Frequency builds the response; duration does not. One long session a day is not the same thing and will not do the same thing.

    Nothing happening is normal

    Expect nothing for the first few days. It usually appears somewhere between a few days and two weeks, and may arrive as warmth, tingling, sinking, or the arm feeling larger or further away — those all count.

    If restlessness or emotion comes up

    That is a recognised part of the process — the old books call it an autogenic discharge — and not a sign that something is wrong. If it is uncomfortable: open your eyes, move your arms and legs, and stop there for that session.

    New to the method?

    Autogenic training was worked out in Berlin in the 1920s and taught ever since. You repeat a short phrase to yourself — my right arm is heavy — and let it be true rather than making it true. Do that often enough and the shift becomes something you can reach for deliberately, which is the whole point: not to relax now, but to be able to.

    What people notice

    Less noise in the head — not silence, the volume down. Coming down faster after something stressful. Settling into sleep. A cleaner head between two demanding things. What arrives, and when, varies a lot between people.

    This is a self-training method, not medical treatment or advice. If something is wrong, see a doctor.

    Before you practise

    Never while driving, cycling, or doing anything that needs your attention. Deep relaxation can leave you light-headed when you get up — if you have low blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, or epilepsy, speak to your doctor before making it a daily habit.

    Always finish by waking yourself up: bend and stretch your arms firmly, fists clenched; two deep breaths; open your eyes. About fifteen seconds, and it is what stops you feeling groggy. The one exception is practising in bed to fall asleep — then stay lying down. (Traditionally this is called the take-back.)

    Before you start

    What this is, and what it isn't.

    Autogenic training is a self-training method: you learn deliberate access to a state your body normally only reaches on its own. This app teaches it and keeps a record of your practice.

    It is not medical treatment and nothing here is medical advice. It does not diagnose or treat any condition. If something is wrong, see a doctor — this sits alongside that, never instead of it.

    Deep relaxation can leave you light-headed when you stand up. If you have low blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, or epilepsy, speak to your doctor before making this a daily habit. Every practice ends by waking you up again, and the app always runs that part. Two of the later exercises are ones to learn in person rather than from a recording, and it will tell you when you reach them.
