Stage two. Same ninety seconds — this time both arms, one after the other.

Sit back, or lie down. Arms a little away from your sides.

Close your eyes.

Silently, in your own voice.

I am completely calm.

Your right arm first.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

Now the left arm.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

I am completely calm.

Now the take-back.

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

Take a deep breath in — and out.

And open your eyes.

That's the stage. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

Stay with this one until the heaviness turns up by itself in most practices. Usually about a week.

Then move on. Little, and often.
