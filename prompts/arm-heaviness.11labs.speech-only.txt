Welcome. Find your position now — sitting with your head and arms supported, or lying on your back with your arms a little away from your sides.

Let your hands rest with the palms down, the fingers loosely curled. Let your feet fall outward.

When you are settled, let your eyes close.

There is nothing to achieve in the next few minutes. Nothing to get right. You are simply going to rest your attention on a few quiet phrases, and allow whatever happens to happen.

Notice the weight of your body where it is supported. The chair, or the floor, is holding you completely.

And notice your breathing, without changing it. Let it come and go by itself.

Say quietly, inwardly, with me:

I am completely calm.

I am completely calm.

Now let your attention rest gently on your right arm.

Not on the idea of your arm — on the arm itself. The upper arm. The elbow. The forearm. The hand as it lies there.

You are not going to make your arm heavy. You are simply going to notice that it already is. Let the words pass through your mind slowly, and then let go of them.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

I am completely calm.

If your mind has wandered, that is not a mistake. Notice it, and let your attention drift back to the arm, without effort.

The weight of the arm. Sinking. Held.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

My right arm is heavy.

I am completely calm.

Now let your attention move across, to your left arm.

The shoulder. The upper arm. The elbow, the forearm, the hand.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

My left arm is heavy.

I am completely calm.

The arm is heavy, and it is comfortable. Heavy, and warm, and entirely at rest.

And now both arms together.

Both arms are heavy.

Both arms are heavy.

Both arms are heavy.

Both arms are heavy.

Both arms are heavy.

Both arms are heavy.

I am completely calm.

The heaviness may spread a little on its own — into the shoulders, across the chest, down into the legs. Let it go wherever it goes. You do not need to direct it.

My arms are heavy. I am completely calm.

My arms are heavy. I am completely calm.

Now let the words fall away altogether.

Rest here, with nothing to do, and nothing to say.

Breathing quietly. The body heavy, and held.

I am completely calm.

In a moment we will finish, and you will bring the heaviness to an end.

First: bend and stretch your arms. Clench your fists and pull them in towards your shoulders — firmly, several times.

Keep going. Feel the strength coming back into the arms.

Now take a deep breath in — and out.

And once more, a full breath in — and out.

Now stretch, if you would like to. And open your eyes.

The exercise is finished. Take a moment before you stand.

Well done. I will see you at the next practice.
