Step six. Heaviness, warmth, heartbeat — then the breath.

Settle. Eyes closed.

Silently, in your own voice.

I am completely calm.

Do not take a breath. Let one be taken.

My arms are heavy.

My arms are warm.

My heartbeat is calm and regular.

It breathes me.

It breathes me.

It breathes me.

It breathes me.

I am completely calm.

Now the take-back.

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

A deep breath in — and out.

And open your eyes.

That's step 6. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

Stay with it until it turns up without your help. Then add the next one.
