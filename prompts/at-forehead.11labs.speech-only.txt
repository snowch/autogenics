Step eight. The full sequence, and then the last formula.

Settle. Eyes closed.

Silently, in your own voice.

I am completely calm.

As though a window had been opened somewhere in the room.

My arms are heavy.

My arms are warm.

My heartbeat is calm and regular.

It breathes me.

My solar plexus is warm.

My forehead is cool.

My forehead is cool.

My forehead is cool.

I am completely calm.

Now the take-back.

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

A deep breath in — and out.

And open your eyes.

That's step 8. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

Stay with it until it turns up without your help. Then add the next one.
