# After your first practice

**~100 seconds · offered once, after the first logged practice**

<!-- render: --speed 1.0 --lead-in 0.4 -->

This is the half of the old explainer that works far better *after* the first
attempt than before it. "What was I supposed to feel?" is a question nobody
really has until they have sat there for ninety seconds and, most likely, felt
nothing at all. Delivered four minutes before that, it is abstract; delivered
straight afterwards, it is the answer to a question the user is actually
holding.

So it is not shown at first run. It appears on the practice screen once the
first practice is logged, as an offer rather than an interruption — the user may
be about to get up and go to work, and a film that seizes the moment after a
relaxation exercise would undo the exercise.

What stays *ahead* of practice one is only what would otherwise be done wrong:
hold the phrase lightly, do not try. Both are already in the first-run film and
in the guided track itself.

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

That's one practice. Here is what it was, and what to look for next time.

[pause 0.9]

First: if nothing happened, nothing has gone wrong. For most people it is a few
days, sometimes two weeks, before anything turns up at all.

[pause 0.9]

Here is why heaviness in particular.

[pause 0.7]

When your body drops out of alert mode, your muscles let go of the low
background tension they have been holding all day. Heaviness is what that
letting-go feels like from the inside.

[pause 0.9]

So the heaviness is not really the goal. It is the signal that the shift has
happened.

[pause 0.9]

And it may not arrive as heaviness exactly.

[pause 0.6]

Warmth, tingling, a sinking feeling, the arm seeming larger, or further away
than it is. Those all count.

[pause 0.9]

Occasionally you get the opposite. Restlessness, a twitch, an urge to move, or
unexpected emotion.

[pause 0.7]

That has a name — an autogenic discharge — and it is a recognised part of the
process, not a sign that anything is wrong.

[pause 0.7]

If it is uncomfortable, open your eyes, move your arms and legs, and stop there
for that session.

[pause 0.9]

One last thing, and it is the one that catches everybody. You cannot make this
happen by trying. Effort is the single thing that reliably blocks it.

[pause 0.9]

That is everything. Twice more today.

<!-- narration:end -->
