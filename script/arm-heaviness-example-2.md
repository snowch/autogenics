# Autogenic Training — Both Arms, One at a Time (Example 2)

**Second stage · ~90 seconds of practice · stage two of three**

Stage two of the first standard exercise: the dominant arm, then the other. Same ninety seconds — the second arm joins, it does not lengthen the practice.

Like [example one](arm-heaviness-example.md), this is a track to learn the
shape from, not to play daily. Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three
times a day, no audio.

## Moving on

Stay at this stage until the heaviness arrives by itself in roughly two
practices out of three, across three days running — and give it at least five
days regardless. Do not advance on the strength of one good session.

## Production notes

- Practice at session pace; `[speed 1.12]` returns the cancellation and
  handover to normal speed.
- The formulas stay near-monotone and identical each time.

Stage two. Same ninety seconds — this time both arms, one after the other.

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

> Brisk and awake from here.

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

Take a deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

That's the stage. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

[pause 1.5]

Stay with this one until the heaviness turns up by itself in most practices.
Usually about a week.

[pause 1.5]

Then move on. Little, and often.
