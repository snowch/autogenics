# Autogenic Training — Arm Heaviness (Example)

**One practice, done with you · ~2:30 · the daily dose, demonstrated**

This is not a track to play every day. It is one complete practice at the
length you should actually be doing — about ninety seconds — done *with* you
rather than at you, and then handed over.

Listen to it a handful of times, until you know the shape. After that, practise
on your own: three times a day, ninety seconds, no audio. That is the point of
this recording, and it says so out loud at the end.

---

## Why this exists

The full and short sessions are 14:32 and 7:10. Schultz's protocol is roughly
30–90 seconds per exercise, **three times a day** — frequency is what builds
the response, not duration. A fourteen-minute session played once daily is the
wrong dose in both directions.

An example short enough to reproduce from memory is also the only kind worth
calling an example. This one is.

## What it models

- Dominant arm only. In the first week that is the whole exercise; the other
  limbs come later.
- Six repetitions, roughly four seconds apart.
- The frame formula before and after.
- The cancellation, at full length.

## The handover

The teaching is deliberately placed **after** the cancellation, not before or
during. Structural instruction mid-exercise breaks passive concentration — but
once the take-back has been done the listener is awake on purpose, which makes
it the one safe place to say "now do this yourself."

## Production notes

- Practice runs at the session defaults. `[speed 1.12]` at the cancellation
  re-synthesises the take-back and handover slightly faster, so they wake the
  listener rather than drifting on at induction pace. Done at the model rather
  than by resampling, which leaves no artefacts on a low voice.
- Voice: same as the sessions, for continuity.

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

> Brisk and awake from here. The exercise is over.

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

Take a deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

That was the whole thing.

[pause 1]

Ninety seconds. One phrase, six times, and the take-back at the end. Nothing
you need me for.

[pause 1.5]

So do that yourself — three times today. Morning, midday, evening. Sitting at
your desk is fine.

[pause 1.5]

Come back to this recording only if you forget the shape of it. You won't need
it for long.
