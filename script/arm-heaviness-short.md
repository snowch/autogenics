# Autogenic Training — Arm Heaviness (Short)

**First Standard Exercise (Heaviness) · young-adult version · ~7 minutes**

A condensed version of the [full session](arm-heaviness.md), for people who
want to actually do this twice a day rather than admire it once. Same
technique, same formulas — fewer repetitions, tighter pauses, and language that
doesn't sound like a clinic.

---

## Before you begin

- Sit somewhere you can let your head rest back, or lie on your back with your
  arms a little away from your sides.
- Put your phone face down, or somewhere you can't see it.
- **Not while driving, cycling, or anything else that needs you.**
- This lowers your heart rate and blood pressure a little. If you have low
  blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes, or epilepsy, check with your
  doctor before making it a habit.
- If you feel restless, twitchy or tearful partway through, that's a known
  thing — it's called an autogenic discharge, and it isn't a sign you're doing
  it wrong. Open your eyes, move your arms and legs, and stop there.
- **Don't stop the recording before the end.** The last 40 seconds bring you
  back up, and skipping them can leave you groggy.

## How to practise

Twice a day beats once a week. Two minutes of this done regularly does more
than an hour done occasionally.

Nothing may happen for the first several sessions. That's normal and not a
sign you're bad at it — the heaviness turns up on its own, and trying to force
it is the one thing that reliably prevents it.

> **Left-handed:** swap "right" and "left" throughout. You always start with
> your dominant arm.

## What's different from the full session

| | Full | Short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Length | 14:32 | ~7:00 |
| Formula repetitions | 6 per limb | 4 per limb |
| Pause between repetitions | 8s | 5s |
| Rest phase | ~1:40 | ~25s |
| Cancellation | full | full — never shortened |

Four repetitions instead of six is a deliberate departure from Schultz. It
keeps the session short enough to actually repeat daily, which matters more
than orthodoxy at the start. Move to the full version once the heaviness comes
reliably.

## Production notes

- Voice: calm and level, warm but not soothing-at-you. Talk to the listener,
  not over them.
- The formulas are near-monotone and identical every time. Don't perform them.
- The cancellation is the one part that speeds up — clear and awake, not loud.

[pause 7]

### Cancellation

[stretch 1.0]

We're going to finish. This last part brings you back up, so do it properly.

This resets your muscles and circulation to normal waking tone. <!-- safety -->

[pause 4]

## Cancellation formula (for reference)

1. **Arms firm** — flex and extend, fists clenched.
2. **Breathe deeply** — two or three full breaths.
3. **Open eyes.**

Never skip it, and never end a session sitting up without it.
