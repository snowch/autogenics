# Autogenic Training — Arm Heaviness

**First Standard Exercise (Heaviness) · Schultz protocol · ~14 minutes**

A guided session for the first of the six standard autogenic exercises. The
practitioner learns to induce the sensation of heaviness in the arms through
*passive concentration* — resting attention on a formula without trying to make
anything happen.

---

## Before you begin

- Sit in an armchair with your head supported, or lie on your back with your
  arms slightly away from your body. Loosen anything tight at the waist, neck
  or wrists.
- **Do not listen while driving or operating machinery.**
- Autogenic training lowers blood pressure and heart rate. If you have low
  blood pressure, a heart condition, diabetes (it can affect insulin
  requirements), epilepsy, or you are in treatment for a psychotic disorder,
  speak to your clinician before practising regularly.
- If at any point you feel uncomfortable, restless or distressed, stop, open
  your eyes and move your arms and legs. That is a normal *autogenic
  discharge*, not a failure.
- **The cancellation at the end is part of the exercise.** Do not skip it,
  and do not stop the recording early — unless you are using this to fall
  asleep, in which case simply let the session end and stay lying down.

## How to practise

Do this two or three times a day, in the same posture each time. The first
week is only about heaviness in the arms; resist the temptation to add the
other formulas. Heaviness may take several sessions to appear, and it appears
by itself — never by effort.

> **Left-handed practitioners:** substitute "left" for "right" throughout the
> first block, and "right" for "left" in the second. The dominant arm is
> always trained first.

## Production notes

- Voice: calm, low, unhurried, warm but neutral — a companion, not a performer.
  Slightly slower than conversational, with a falling intonation at the end of
  each formula.
- Formulas are repeated in groups of six, in a near-monotone. Repetition should
  sound identical each time; do not "act" the repetitions.
- The silences are the exercise. Do not shorten them.
- Approximate durations: settling 2:00 · dominant arm 3:00 · other arm 2:30 ·
  both arms 2:30 · rest 2:00 · cancellation 0:45.

## Cancellation formula (for reference)

The classical "take-back" (*Zurücknahme*), in order, is:

1. **Arms firm** — flex and extend the arms vigorously, fists clenched.
2. **Breathe deeply** — two or three full breaths.
3. **Open eyes.**

Never omit it, and never leave the session sitting up without it.
