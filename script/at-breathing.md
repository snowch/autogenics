# Autogenic Training — Breathing

**The fourth standard exercise · ~90 seconds · step 7**

The one formula written in the passive, on purpose: breathing is watched, never steered.

Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three times a day, no audio. This
recording is here so you know the shape, not to be played daily.


## About this one

"It breathes me" is deliberately odd. The grammar is doing the work: the moment you start managing your breathing, the exercise has stopped.

## Moving on

Stay here until it arrives by itself in roughly two practices out of three
across three days running, and at least five days regardless.

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

A deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

[pause 1.5]
