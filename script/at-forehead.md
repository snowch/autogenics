# Autogenic Training — Cool head

**The sixth standard exercise · ~90 seconds**

The only one that goes the other way: everything else warms and settles, this stays clear.

Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three times a day, no audio. This
recording is here so you know the shape, not to be played daily.

> **Caution.** Learned last, and gently. If you are prone to headaches or migraine, keep it brief — or leave this one out and stay with the first five.

## About this one

Cool rather than cold. Overdone, this exercise can bring on a headache in people prone to them; a light touch is the whole technique.

## Moving on

Stay here until it arrives by itself in roughly two practices out of three
across three days running, at least seven days regardless, and you can hold the
sequence once with nothing playing and nothing on screen.

The full sequence, and then the last formula.

[pause 3]

[pause 3]

My forehead is cool.

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

A deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

That's it. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

[pause 1.5]

Stay with it until it turns up without your help. Then add the next one.
