# Autogenic Training — Heartbeat

**The third standard exercise · ~90 seconds · step 5**

Attention rests on the heartbeat without altering it. Notice it; leave it alone.

Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three times a day, no audio. This
recording is here so you know the shape, not to be played daily.

> **Caution.** This exercise is traditionally taught with a trained instructor rather than from a recording. If you have any heart condition, learn it in person, not here.

## About this one

If watching the heartbeat makes you anxious, stop and stay with heaviness and warmth. That reaction is common and is a reason to work with an instructor.

## Moving on

Stay here until it arrives by itself in roughly two practices out of three
across three days running, and at least five days regardless.

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

A deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

That's step 5. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

[pause 1.5]
