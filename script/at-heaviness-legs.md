# Autogenic Training — Heaviness, legs

**Completes the first standard exercise · ~90 seconds · step 4**

The arms were the training ground. This is where the response generalises to
the rest of the body, and it is where the classical formula lands: *my arms and
legs are heavy*.

Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three times a day, no audio. This
recording is here so you know the shape, not to be played daily.


## Why this step exists at all

The app used to go straight from both arms to warmth, which quietly dropped
half of the first standard exercise. Heaviness in autogenic training is a
*limb* response, not an arm response — the standard texts describe it arriving
in the arms and legs, and the consolidated formula names both.

## Why one step and not three

The arms take three steps — right, left, both — because that is where the skill
is learned: holding a phrase without effort, and noticing rather than
producing. Once that is in place the response generalises, and the literature
describes it doing so within about a week. Repeating the full right-left-both
scaffold for the legs would teach nothing new and add a fortnight to the ladder
for no gain. One step, both legs, then the consolidation.

The same reasoning is already why warmth is a single step rather than three.

## Moving on

Stay here until the legs come in by themselves in roughly two practices out of
three across three days running, and at least five days regardless.

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

### Practice

The arms are yours now. The legs come the same way — and mostly they
come on their own.

[pause 2]

Settle. Eyes closed.

[pause 3]

Silently, in your own voice.

[pause 2]

I am completely calm.

[pause 3]

Let the arms go heavy first. You do not have to work for it.

[pause 3]

My arms are heavy.

[pause 4]

My legs are heavy.

[pause 4]

My legs are heavy.

[pause 4]

My legs are heavy.

[pause 4]

My arms and legs are heavy.

[pause 5]

I am completely calm.

[pause 5]

### Cancellation

[speed 1.12]

Now the take-back.

[pause 1]

Bend and stretch your arms. Fists clenched, pull them in firmly, several times.

[pause 5]

A deep breath in — and out.

[pause 4]

And open your eyes.

[pause 3]

### Handover

That's step 4. Ninety seconds, three times a day, on your own.

[pause 1.5]

Stay with it until the legs turn up without your help. Then add the next one.
