# Autogenic Training — Warm centre

**The fifth standard exercise · ~90 seconds · step 8**

Warmth through the upper abdomen, behind the stomach.

Practise it yourself: ninety seconds, three times a day, no audio. This
recording is here so you know the shape, not to be played daily.

> **Caution.** Traditionally taught with a trained instructor. Skip it, and learn it in person, if you have diabetes, a stomach ulcer, any abdominal condition, or are pregnant.

## About this one

This one has the strongest tradition of in-person teaching behind it. Treat the recording as a description of the exercise, not a substitute for being taught it.

## Moving on

Stay here until it arrives by itself in roughly two practices out of three
across three days running, and at least five days regardless.
