# Briefing — Breathing

**~70 seconds · offered when you reach it, before its first practice**

<!-- render: --speed 1.1 --lead-in 0.4 -->

Each new exercise introduces itself at the moment it starts, rather than being
explained weeks earlier in a single upfront lecture nobody can hold. This is the
"what does this one actually do" film for this step.

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

New exercise, and the phrase is deliberately strange.

[pause 0.9]

It breathes me.

[pause 0.9]

Not "I am breathing calmly". The odd grammar is the instruction: the breath is
being done, and you are watching it.

[pause 0.9]

This is the exercise people most often get wrong, because breathing is the one
autonomic thing you can seize control of — so you will, without meaning to.

[pause 0.9]

You will notice you are steering it. That is not a failure — that is the
exercise working.

[pause 0.7]

Notice, let go of the wheel, and go back to watching.

[pause 0.9]

Do not deepen it, slow it, or count it. Ninety seconds, three times a day.

<!-- narration:end -->
