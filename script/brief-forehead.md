# Briefing — Cool head

**~70 seconds · offered when you reach step 9, before its first practice**

<!-- render: --speed 1.0 --lead-in 0.4 -->

Each new exercise introduces itself at the moment it starts, rather than being
explained weeks earlier in a single upfront lecture nobody can hold. This is the
"what does this one actually do" film for step 9.

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

Last exercise, and the only one that goes the other way.

[pause 0.9]

My forehead is cool.

[pause 0.9]

Everything so far has been settling — heavy, warm, slow. This one stays clear.
The body quiet, the head not foggy.

[pause 0.9]

That contrast is the point. It is what separates this from drifting off, and it
is why the full sequence leaves you rested rather than groggy.

[pause 0.9]

Cool, not cold. A slight freshness across the forehead, as if a window were
open somewhere. Keep it light and keep it brief.

[pause 0.9]

If you are prone to headaches or migraine, keep it very brief — or leave this
one out and stay with the first five. That is a complete practice.

<!-- narration:end -->
