# Briefing — Heartbeat

**~80 seconds · offered when you reach it, before its first practice**

<!-- render: --speed 1.0 --lead-in 0.4 -->

Each new exercise introduces itself at the moment it starts, rather than being
explained weeks earlier in a single upfront lecture nobody can hold. This is the
"what does this one actually do" film for this step.

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

New exercise, and the one to take most gently.

[pause 0.9]

The phrase is: my heartbeat is calm and regular.

[pause 0.8]

Your attention rests on the beat. It does not adjust it. You are not slowing
anything down — noticing it is the whole exercise, and the settling follows on
its own.

[pause 0.9]

Find it wherever it is easiest. The chest, the neck, the wrist, or just a
general sense of it. You do not need a hand on your chest.

[pause 0.9]

Two things before you start.

[pause 0.7]

This is one of the exercises traditionally taught in person rather than from a
recording. If you have any heart condition, learn it with an instructor
instead.

[pause 0.9]

And if attending to your own heartbeat makes you uneasy — which is common, and
not a flaw — leave this step out.

[pause 0.7]

Stay with heaviness and warmth. They are the two that do most of the work.

<!-- narration:end -->
