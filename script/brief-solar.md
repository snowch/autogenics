# Briefing — Warm centre

**~75 seconds · offered when you reach step 8, before its first practice**

<!-- render: --speed 1.0 --lead-in 0.4 -->

Each new exercise introduces itself at the moment it starts, rather than being
explained weeks earlier in a single upfront lecture nobody can hold. This is the
"what does this one actually do" film for step 8.

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

New exercise. Warmth again, but further in.

[pause 0.9]

The phrase is: my solar plexus is warm.

[pause 0.8]

The solar plexus is the soft area of the upper abdomen, just below where the
ribs meet.

[pause 0.7]

Warmth there is the digestive side of the body coming back online — which it
only does once you are genuinely not braced.

[pause 0.9]

That makes this one a fair test of everything before it. If the earlier steps
are not landing, this will not either, and the answer is to go back rather than
to push.

[pause 0.9]

This is the second of the exercises traditionally taught in person.

[pause 0.7]

Skip it and learn it with an instructor if you have diabetes, a stomach ulcer,
any abdominal condition, or you are pregnant.

[pause 0.8]

Otherwise: settle, run the earlier phrases, then add this one.

<!-- narration:end -->
