# Finished

**~70 seconds · offered once, when the last step's own record says it has landed**

<!-- render: --speed 1.1 --lead-in 0.4 -->

The first screen promises that you end up not needing this. Until now nothing
ever said so — the last step simply ran forever, with no acknowledgement that
the thing had been learned. A product that sells its own ending has to be
willing to reach it.

It is deliberately not a celebration. No confetti, no badge, no streak saved.
The tone is a handover: you have the response, it is yours, and the app is now
optional. Anything more triumphant would be at odds with a method whose entire
skill is not making a fuss.

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

That is the whole method. Six exercises, and you have all of them.

[pause 0.9]

Which means the useful thing now is not this app. It is that you can do this
anywhere, with nothing.

[pause 0.9]

On a train. Between two meetings. In a waiting room, or lying awake at two in
the morning.

[pause 0.9]

Ninety seconds, eyes closed or not, with nobody guiding you. That was always
the point.

[pause 0.9]

Keep it up, though. This is a trained response, and like anything trained it
fades if it is never used.

[pause 0.7]

Most people settle into once or twice a day, or reach for it when they need it.
Either is fine. Stopping completely, for months, is what undoes it.

[pause 0.9]

You are welcome to keep logging here — it is how you notice a slip before it
becomes a stop. But you do not need to.

[pause 0.9]

That is everything. Go and use it.
