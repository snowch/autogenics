# Segments — the formulae

**~25 seconds each · one per stage, stacked in order**

<!-- render: --speed 0.90 --lead-in 0.4 -->

One segment per formula. A session plays the ones you have, in order, so the
stack grows with the stage and the session length is derived from it rather
than configured. `{limb}` is templated; every formula after the first two is
limb-neutral already, so only two of these ever need a mirrored recording.

Each has a **lead-in** that plays only while the stage is new and then drops,
and a **body** that is the phrase and the silence after it. The silence is not
padding. Saying the phrase is the smaller half of the exercise; the gap is where
you find out whether anything arrived.

**Screen cue:** the phrase itself, shortening as the stage fades —
`My right arm is heavy.` → `Right arm, heavy.` → `Heavy.`

---

## heavy-arm · Foundation, and the first rung of the ladder

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
Your {limb} arm only. Not both, not the legs. One arm, for as long as it takes.

[pause 0.9]

Say it to yourself, then stop and let the arm answer or not. Either is fine.
Chasing it is the one thing that reliably stops it.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body:

<!-- narration:start -->
My {limb} arm is heavy. [pause 3.5]
My {limb} arm is heavy. [pause 3.5]
My {limb} arm is heavy. [pause 3.5]
My {limb} arm is heavy. [pause 3.5]
My {limb} arm is heavy. [pause 3.5]
<!-- narration:end -->

---

## heavy-all · stage 1

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
Same weight, now named everywhere. The arm taught it; the rest tend to follow
without being taught separately.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `My arms and legs are heavy.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## warm-arm · stage 2

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
Blood, not muscle. Nothing to picture — no fire, no light. Say it, and notice
the hand.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `My {limb} arm is warm.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## warm-all · stage 2, once the arm has it

Body: `My arms are warm.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## heartbeat · stage 3

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
Find the beat wherever it is easiest. You are not slowing it. Noticing it is the
whole exercise, and the settling happens on its own or not at all.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `My heartbeat is calm and regular.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## breath · stage 4

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
The grammar is deliberate. Breathing is the one of these you can take over
without meaning to — so you will. Notice, let go of it, go back to watching.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `It breathes me.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## solar · stage 5 · learn in person

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
Warmth again, further in — the soft place under the ribs. This one tests
everything before it. If the earlier ones are not landing, this will not either,
and the answer is to go back rather than push.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `My solar plexus is warm.` × 5, 3.5s apart.

---

## forehead · stage 6

Lead-in:

<!-- narration:start -->
The only one that goes the other way. Everything so far has been settling; this
stays clear. Cool, not cold — keep it light and keep it brief.
<!-- narration:end -->

Body: `My forehead is cool.` × 5, 3.5s apart.
