# Segment — posture

**~15 seconds · structure · fires at the start of every session**

<!-- render: --speed 0.90 --lead-in 0.4 -->

The container is fixed and never fades. This segment always runs; only the
narration inside it drops away. On screen it is one line, and once the voice is
gone that line is the whole of it.

Permissive about where, definite about the hands. Palms down and resting is the
one detail that matters, because a held hand is a hand still working — and the
first formula is about the arm.

**Screen cue:** `Sit back, or lie down.`

---

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

Sit back, or lie down.

[pause 1.5]

Let your hands rest where they fall, palms down. Resting, not placed.

[pause 1.5]

Close your eyes. If you would rather not, let them rest half open, looking at
nothing in particular.

[pause 1.5]

Nothing else to arrange.

<!-- narration:end -->
