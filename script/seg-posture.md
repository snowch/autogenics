# Segment — posture

**~15 seconds · structure · fires at the start of every session**

<!-- render: --speed 0.90 --lead-in 0.4 -->

The container is fixed and never fades. This segment always runs; only the
narration inside it drops away. On screen it is one line, and once the voice is
gone that line is the whole of it.

Permissive about where, definite about the hands. Palms down and resting is the
one detail that matters, because a held hand is a hand still working — and the
first formula is about the arm.

**Screen cue:** `Sit back, or lie down.`

---

## The shrink ladder

Not taught-or-gone. Three rungs at eight and twenty repetitions, which is the
app's existing convention — `shrink(full, mid, mature)`, `shrinkCue`, and every
drill's three-element cue array already work exactly this way.

Position was the layer that made this obvious: once you know it, it does not
disappear, it becomes one line. *Get into position, then we scan.* The segment
still runs its fifteen seconds either way, because getting into position takes
as long as it takes whether or not you are being told how. The container never
fades; only the words do.

| reps | what is said |
| --- | --- |
| under 8 | the full narration below |
| 8–19 | **Into position. Hands where they fall.** |
| 20+ | **Position.** |

## Narration

<!-- narration:start -->

Sit back, or lie down.

[pause 1.5]

Let your hands rest where they fall, palms down. Resting, not placed.

[pause 1.5]

Close your eyes. If you would rather not, let them rest half open, looking at
nothing in particular.

[pause 1.5]

Nothing else to arrange.

<!-- narration:end -->
